PRIVACY POLICY

PRISMFORGE Audio-Reactive Visualizer

Last updated: October 20, 2026

PRISMFORGE is an audio-reactive visualizer made by BasicGlitch. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short, PRISMFORGE does not collect, store, or send your personal data.

What the app does with audio

PRISMFORGE can read audio in three ways:

  • Audio file: You choose a file on your device. The app plays it and draws visuals from it.
  • Microphone: With your permission, the app reads sound from the microphone.
  • System audio: With your permission, the app reads audio that another app is playing. Android asks you to approve screen capture each time. The app uses only the audio, not the screen contents.

The app analyzes this audio in memory to drive the visuals. It does not record the audio to a file, and it does not send the audio anywhere.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting.

Data stored on your device

The app can save files only when you choose to:

  • Presets: Saving a preset writes a small settings file to your device.
  • Frames: Saving a frame writes an image file to your device.

These files stay on your device. The app does not upload them.

Data sent off your device

The app does not send data to us or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only for the microphone and system audio options described above. You can deny it or revoke it in Android settings.
  • Screen capture (MediaProjection): Used only to read system audio. The app stops capture when you stop it or close the app.

Children

PRISMFORGE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
