5 PIECES IN THIS SERIES

Munch, Van Gogh, Warhol, Da Vinci, and Grant Wood, all run through the Broboticus filter. The Screambot stands on Munch's bridge receiving too many frequencies at once, a vinyl player where the dread should be. Marilyn Monbroe replaces Warhol's face with a BROBOTICUS chest panel across six panels of oversized multicolor lips. Vitruvian Broboticus holds vinyl and headphones where Da Vinci's man holds nothing, and the proportions check out. American Gothbotic swaps Grant Wood's pitchfork for a wrench; the woman has seen stranger things. Vincent VanBoticus collapses Van Gogh's Sunflowers and Starry Night into one broadcast tower. These are not parodies. They are parallel arguments.
