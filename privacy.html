This Privacy Policy describes how BasicGlitch ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit basicglitch.art or interact with our services.

We may collect the following types of information:

How We Use Your Information

Email Communications

We use Gumroad to manage our email list. By subscribing, you agree to receive emails from BasicGlitch. You can unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email. We follow CAN-SPAM Act requirements and respect your unsubscribe requests immediately.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at our contact page.
