BROBOTOSAURUS WREX

COLLECTION: BROBOTICUS · CASE STUDY 42

Every case study has an origin, and this is Broboticus at full amplitude — rendered in full comic book color rather than signature neon line work. He's astride a saddled T-Rex, reins in one hand, a vintage coiled-cord microphone raised in the other like he's calling the whole thing into the record. Flying saucers trail smoke across a burning orange and purple skyline. Brobotosaurus Wrex is the piece that establishes the scale of this character — not a robot doing leisure activities, but a robot whose leisure activities happen to involve dinosaurs, spacecraft, and the fall of civilization. Field Report 00.
