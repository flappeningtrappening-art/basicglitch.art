CUBIST CHEF

COLLECTION: MASTERS REMIXED

Chefboticus gets the Picasso treatment: fractured planes, burnt sienna and terracotta, a rolling pin in hand and the New York skyline through the kitchen window. He is unbothered by the Chrysler Building. The dough demands focus. Two visual languages, one kitchen: the robot's form shattered into warm geometric facets while Manhattan remains in a deliberately flatter graphic style behind him.
